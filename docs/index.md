Houston ISD is Texas's largest school district by enrollment, serving roughly 167,000 students across 274 schools. It is governed by a state-appointed Board of Managers: the Texas Education Agency took over the district's governance on June 1, 2023, replacing the elected nine-member Board of Education, after years of a low academic accountability rating at one campus and findings of governance misconduct by the prior elected board. TEA has since extended the intervention through June 1, 2027. The district's budget and financial planning office prepares its annual budget, adopted by the Board of Managers each June; HISD's Controller's Office publishes its audited financial statements. Like all Texas school districts, HISD is funded through the state's Foundation School Program formula (administered by TEA) plus local property taxes, and — because HISD's local property wealth per student exceeds the state-set entitlement in most years — is subject to "recapture," under which some of its local tax revenue is sent to the state for redistribution to property-poor districts.
