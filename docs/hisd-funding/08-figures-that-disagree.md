# 8. Figures that disagree across sources

HISD's own documents, and HISD's figures against TEA's and the bond market's, disagree with each other on several points. They are listed here rather than silently picking one. The second edition (Oct. 8, 2026) explained the two recapture rows by pulling TEA's full revision history; the rest remain open. See [the audit summary](audit-summary.md).

| Item | Values found | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FY2025 recapture paid | $49.1M (TEA Summary of Finances, Final); $56.9M (audited ACFR and TEA's PEIMS actuals); $44.5M (HISD's own final amended budget); approximately $65M (a bond official statement); $55.5M (a Texas Tribune news report) | **Largely explained (second edition).** TEA re-issued the 2024-25 report 41 times. The ~$65M matches TEA's preliminary runs of Sept. 2024–Feb. 2025 ($64.1–64.9M); the $55.5M matches the Near-Final runs of Sept. 19 and Oct. 17, 2025 exactly; the audited $56.9M matches the run current when HISD closed its books (Sept. 30, 2025: $55.5M) within $1.4M; $49.1M is the final settlement. Only HISD's $44.5M budget figure matches no TEA run. See [`notes/sof_history.md`](notes/sof_history.md). |
| FY2024 recapture paid | $0 (ACFR and PEIMS) versus $326.5M budgeted in HISD's original FY2024 budget | **Explained (second edition).** HISD adopted its FY2024 budget in June 2023, when TEA's first projection showed $335.2M of recapture for 2023-24. The November 2023 tax-compression law (SB 2, 88th Legislature, 2nd Called Session) reduced it to $0. |
