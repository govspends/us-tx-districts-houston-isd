# 5. Budget versus actual results, and reserves

## Adopted budget versus what actually happened

| General Fund | FY2025 | FY2026 | FY2027 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Adopted revenue | $1,888.6M | $2,082.0M | $1,990.4M |
| Adopted spending | $2,100.3M | $2,121.9M | $2,015.0M |
| Adopted operating deficit | −$211.7M | −$39.9M | −$24.6M |
| Budgeted property sales + transfers | +$80.6M | +$25.2M | +$23.8M |
| **Budgeted change in fund balance** | **−$131.1M** | **−$14.7M** | **−$0.8M** |
| Actual change in fund balance | **−$299.1M** (audited) | −$18.0M per the June 2026 amendment; projected ending balance $752.0M (unaudited) | — |

<p class="caption">Sources: HISD's adopted budgets and amendments, FY2025-FY2027; audited ACFR for the FY2025 actual.</p>

**FY2025.** HISD budgeted $80 million in revenue from property sales and actually received **$30,000**. Spending ran $74.7 million over the district's own final amended budget, driven by instructional salaries, the Texas Connections Academy virtual school, school leadership, and recapture.

**FY2026.** The adopted budget started from an assumed $799.9 million beginning fund balance. The audited figure for the prior year turned out to be $748.1 million — meaning the FY2026 budget overstated its own starting reserves by about $52 million. The FY2026 budget is also propped up by a one-time $46 million "prior period property value audit" revenue item.

**FY2027 changes** (adopted June 25, 2026):

| Line item | Change from FY2026 |
|---|---:|
| Instruction | **−$96.5M** |
| School leadership | −$10.0M |
| Central office | −$35M |
| NES campus budgets | −$29M |
| School consolidation (closures) | −$15M |
| Transportation | +$12.1M |
| Utilities | +$13.5M |

The FY2027 tax rate drops to $0.8421 per $100 of property value (maintenance & operations $0.6754 plus debt service $0.1667).

## Reserves (General Fund balance, audited)

| June 30 | Total fund balance | Unassigned portion | Months of spending (total / unassigned) |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 2022 | $1,126.9M | $664.2M | 6.8 / 4.0 |
| 2023 | $1,127.1M | $590.1M | 6.2 / 3.3 |
| 2024 | $1,047.2M | **$590.1M** (identical to 2023) | 5.9 / 3.3 |
| 2025 | **$748.1M** | **$464.8M** | **4.0 / 2.5** |
| 2027 (as budgeted) | $729.3M | — | ~4.3 (total) |

- **The unassigned balance was held artificially flat for two years** — exactly $590,067,862 in both 2023 and 2024 — while an "Instructional Reserve" accounting assignment absorbed the real losses, falling from $264.2 million to $215.6 million to $0. Looking at the unassigned figure alone, without also watching the assigned and total balances, would have missed the real drawdown.
- **HISD board policy requires an unassigned balance of at least 3 months of operating spending.** At June 2025 it was 2.48 months — below policy. HISD's own calculation of what 3 months represents is roughly $518-548 million.
- **A widely repeated figure that HISD's reserves "fell about $450 million" is directionally right but matches no single audited line.** Depending on the measure: total General Fund balance fell $379 million; the unrestricted fund balance (committed + assigned + unassigned) fell $501 million; all governmental funds combined fell $546 million.
- HISD's own financial consultants (Alvarez & Marsal) projected the district's fund balance would have fallen to $252 million by FY2026 if no budget cuts had been made.
