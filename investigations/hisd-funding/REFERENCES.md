# References

Sources were retrieved between 2026-10-06 and 2026-10-08; each track's catalog gives the exact retrieval date and URL of every file: `sources/acfr/SOURCES.md`, `sources/budget/SOURCES.md`, `sources/federal_debt/SOURCES.md`, `sources/tea_peims/SOURCES.md`, and `notes/sof_history.md` for TEA's Summary of Finances. "Sources and files" lists every file by folder, with its size and checksum.

## How this page is organized

Sources are listed **by HISD fiscal year** (July 1 to June 30; FY2025 = school year 2024-25). A document is listed under the fiscal year whose figures it reports. For example, the FY2027 budget is under FY2027 even though the board adopted it in June 2026, and the 2025 property-value study is under FY2026 because tax year 2025 funds FY2026. Datasets that span many years are listed once, under "Multi-year sources".

The **Status** column says what kind of figure the document holds:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Audited | HISD's Annual Comprehensive Financial Report (ACFR) or single audit, audited by Weaver and Tidwell, L.L.P. |
| Adopted budget | The budget HISD's board approved before the year began |
| Amended budget | A budget amendment the board approved during the year |
| Budget proposal | Recommended-budget presentations and board workshop decks, including HISD's own projections and forecasts |
| TEA final / TEA estimate | TEA's Summary of Finances: Final settlement, or a Near-Final or Preliminary estimate |
| TEA actual data | Actual spending and revenue that HISD reported to TEA (PEIMS), after year-end |
| TEA budget data | The adopted budget HISD filed with TEA (PEIMS), as TEA publishes it |
| TEA report | TEA's Texas Academic Performance Report (TAPR) or financial-integrity rating (FIRST) |
| Comptroller | Texas Comptroller School District Property Value Study, Final or Preliminary |
| Bond disclosure | HISD's filings with bond investors (EMMA): reported by HISD, not audited except where they reproduce the ACFR |
| HISD web page | A page or announcement on HISD's own website |
| Board policy | A policy adopted by HISD's board |
| Secondary | Press, advocacy or an aggregator; cited by URL (see the last section) |

Every figure in the report pages names its basis. FY2025 is the most recent audited year.
