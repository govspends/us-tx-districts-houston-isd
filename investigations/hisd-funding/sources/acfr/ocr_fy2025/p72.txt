Debt Payable-Governmental Activities

At June 30, 2025, the District had $1,513,635,000 outstanding of general obligation and lease
revenue bonds, and maintenance tax notes. These debt obligations were issued at various dates
from November 1, 2009, through May 1, 2025, with interest rates ranging from 1.00 percent to
6.17 percent, for the purpose of new construction, and equipment of facilities and maintenance of
facilities. All debt obligations fully mature at various dates through February 15, 2043. As of June
30, 2025, the District had no authorized but unissued debt capacity.

Of the District's $1,513,635,000 total debt outstanding at June 30, 2025, $27,505,000 of bonds
payable are attributable to lease revenue bonds issued by the Public Facility Corporation.

In current and prior years, the District legally defeased certain bonds by placing cash and/or
proceeds of refunding bond issues in an irrevocable trust to provide for all future debt service
payments on the refunded debt. Accordingly, the trust account assets and the liability for the
defeased bonds are not included in the District's financial statements. As of June 30, 2025, there
were $115,420,000 in outstanding defeased bonds.

On May 28, 2025, the District defeased $52,705,000 in outstanding par on the Fixed Rate Limited
Tax Schoolhouse Bonds, Series 2017, that were sold with a final maturity date of February 15,
2042. The District utilized available debt service funds for the full redemption of the bond series.
