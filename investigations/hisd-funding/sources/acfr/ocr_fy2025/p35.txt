HOUSTON INDEPENDENT
SCHOOL DISTRICT

Long Term Debt (See Note 8)
As of June 30, 2025, the District had bonded debt outstanding of $1.559 billion. The ratio of net general bonded
debt to assessed valuation and the amount of bonded debt per student are useful indicators of the District's
debt position. This data reflects a decrease in the ratio of net bonded debt to assessed value to 0.55 percent,
as compared to 0.74 percent last year.
The District debt totals included $0 in Variable Rate Bonds outstanding at fiscal year-end.
Outstanding variable-rate debt decreased by $190.435 million from the prior-year ending balance due
to redemptions funded through refunding bonds. In addition, on May 1, 2025, the District redeemed $92.26 million
in fixed-rate bonds as part of the same refunding transaction.
The District executed a targeted debt tender offer during the year ended June 30, 2025, which resulted in the
issuance of $20.55 million in refunding maintenance tax notes. The remaining outstanding balance of previously
issued maintenance tax notes was $143.210 million as of June 30, 2024.
On May 28, 2025, the District defeased $115.42 million in fixed-rate bonds utilizing available debt service funds.
The District did not issue any contractual obligations during the year ended June 30, 2025.
Interest earnings on proceeds from debt issued since May 15, 1989, are subject to arbitrage regulations contained
in the Federal Tax Reform Act of 1986. The District did not identify arbitrage liability as of June 30, 2025.
General Fund Budgetary Highlights
Over the course of the year, the District revised its budget several times. Significant budget adjustments include:

« Decrease in property tax revenues of $24.6 million due to the increase in exemptions and refunds

« Increase in foundation state revenues of $38.5 million

+ Increase in expenditures dues to carryover encumbrances of $17.3 million

« Increase in school leadership of $6.7 million

* Increase in recapture of $44.5 million

« Increase of $47.7 million for facilities maintenance and transportation costs

* Increase in transfers from other funds in the amount of $7.7 million
Total actual expenditures were $74.7 million more than the final budget. Following were the key variances:

+ $59.9 million over budget in instructional salary and Texas Connections expenditures

+ $12.5 million in school leadership

+ $12.4 million in recapture
The 2024-2025 final expenditure budget decreased by $51.0 million from the prior year budget.
The final budgeted amount for revenues was $1.906 billion. Actual revenues were greater than the budget by
$33.8 million primarily due to:

+ $5.3 million under budget in property tax collections

+ $1.5 million over budget in interest earnings

+ $9.9 million over budget in the Foundation School Program

+ $27.9 million over budget in other local sources
