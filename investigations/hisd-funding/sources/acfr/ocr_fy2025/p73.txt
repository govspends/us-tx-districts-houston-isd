HOUSTON INDEPENDENT SCHOOL DISTRICT
NOTES TO THE BASIC FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDED JUNE 30, 2025
Also, on May 28 2025, the District defeased $62,715,000 of outstanding par on the Fixed Rate
Limited Tax Refunding Bonds, Series 2018, that were sold with a final maturity date of February 15,
2043. The District utilized available debt service funds for the full redemption of the bond series.
On March 6, 2025, the District executed a targeted debt tender offer. This tender involved selected
maturities from the Series 2016A, 2017, and 2018 bonds, and was financed through the issuance
of $149,685,000 of Limited Tax Refunding Bond Series 2025A and $20,555,000 of Maintenance
Tax Refunding Note Series 2025 bonds, rather than utilizing available debt service funds. The total
principal amount refunded was $183,780,000, resulting in a debt service savings of $14,764,530
and an economic gain of $10,927,064.
On May 1, 2025, the District redeemed Variable Rate Limited Tax Refunding Bond Series 2014A-2,
Variable Rate Limited Tax Refunding Bond Series 2023C, and Fixed Rate Limited Tax Refunding
Bond Series 2014B bond and was financed through the issuance of Limited Tax Refunding Bond
Series 2025B. The total principal amount refunded was $268,655,000 resulting in a debt service
savings of $57,389,278, and an economic gain of $17,500,810.
As of June 30, 2025, the District had no outstanding variable rate unlimited tax school building bonds.
