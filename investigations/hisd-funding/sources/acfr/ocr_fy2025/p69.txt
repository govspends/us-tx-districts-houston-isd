Impairments
GASB Statement No.42, Accounting and Financial Reporting for Impairment of Capital Assets
and insurance Recoveries, requires the disclosure of impairment loses and associated insurance
recoveries. In fiscal year 2025, the District did not have any significant impairments to be reported.
The District received $3,057,689 in insurance recovery in connection with the Derecho windstorm
of May 2024.
