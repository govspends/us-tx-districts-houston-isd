The Net OPEB liability was measured as of August 31, 2023 and rolled forward to August 31, 2024
and the total OPEB liability used to calculate the Net OPEB liability was determined by an actuarial
valuation as of August 31, 2023. The employer’s proportion of the Net OPEB liability was based on
the employer’s contributions to OPEB relative to the contributions of all employers to the plan for
the period September 1, 2023 through August 31, 2024.
