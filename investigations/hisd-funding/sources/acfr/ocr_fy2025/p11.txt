Operational Improvements

+ Transportation System Overhaul: HISD restructured the transportation department for 2025-26, redesigning
roles and setting new ride-time standards to enhance efficiency and student access.

+ Unified Family Communication Platform: The district launched ParentSquare, consolidating multiple
systems into a single communication platform for parents and guardians.

+ Facilities and Infrastructure Upgrades: Following the defeat of the 2024 bond proposal, HISD approved
approximately $120 million in Maintenance Tax Notes to address priority facility needs, including HVAC,
roofing, technology, and transportation upgrades, and to fund development of the new Career and Technical
Education (CTE) Center at the Grimes Facility.

Governance and Oversight

» The Texas Education Agency (TEA) extended its intervention in HISD through June 2027, with four new
members appointed to the Board of Managers to continue oversight of district reforms and performance
outcomes.

Collectively, these initiatives reflect HISD’s continued progress toward sustainable academic improvement,

governance with long-term strategic goals under Destination 2035.
AWARDS AND ACKNOWLEDGMENTS
Perhaps no responsibility is as great as that of educating our youth, and all of our efforts lead back to the classroom.
Given that the District's academic advancements are dependent upon its fiscal successes, it is imperative that HISD
continue to improve upon its reporting proficiencies. The following text cites awards received by the District for
financial reporting excellence and the related acknowledgments in this regard.
ASBO Certificate of Excellence
The District received the Association of School Business Officials’ (“ASBO”) Certificate of Excellence in Financial
Reporting for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2024. The District has received this award for 46 consecutive years.
This award certifies that the Annual Comprehensive Financial Report substantially conforms to the principles and
standards of financial reporting as recommended and adopted by ASBO.
GFOA Certificate of Achievement
The Government Finance Officers Association of the United States and Canada (“GFOA”) awarded a Certificate
of Achievement for Excellence in Financial Reporting to the District for its Annual Comprehensive Financial Report
for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2024. This was the 52st consecutive year that the District has achieved this
prestigious award.
GTOT Certificate of Distinction
The District received the Government Treasurers’ Organization of Texas (“GTOT”) Certificate of Distinction in
October 2024. The certificate is good for a two-year period. This award recognized the District's commitment to
maintaining a comprehensive written investment policy that meets the criteria set forth in the GTOT Investment
Policy Checklist.
TASBO Award of Merit for Purchasing Operations
The District received the Texas Association of School Business Officials’ (“TASBQO”) Award of Merit of Purchasing
Operations in February 2025. This award honors school districts that uphold professional standards in acquiring
goods and services.
