Benefit Provisions and Service Requirements
TRS administers retirement, disability, and death and survivor benefits to employees and
beneficiaries of employees of the public school system of Texas. The pension formula is calculated
using 2.3 percent times the average of the five highest annual creditable salaries times years of
credited service to arrive at the annual standard annuity except for members who are grandfathered,
the three highest annual salaries are used. Service requirements are as follows:
Normal -- Age 65 with 5 years of credited service, or when the sum of member's age and years of
credit equals or exceeds 80.
Reduced -- Age 55 with at least 5 years of credited service, or earlier than 55 with 30 or more years
of credited service.
Members are fully vested after five years of creditable service and are entitled to any benefit for
which eligibility requirements have been met. There are additional provisions for reduced retirement
if the sum of the member’s age and years of service credit total at least 80, but the member is less
than age 60 or 62 depending on date of employment, or if the member was grandfathered under
a previous rule. For more details about benefits see the Summary of Benefits section of TRS
2024 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report. There are no automatic post-employment benefit
changes, including automatic COLAs. Ad hoc post-employment benefit changes, including ad hoc
COLAs can be granted by the Texas Legislature as noted in the Plan description. The 2023 Texas
Legislature passed Senate Bill (SB) 10 and House Joint Resolution (HJR) 2 to provide eligible
retirees with a one-time stipend and an ad hoc cost-of-living-adjustment (COLA).
One-Time Stipends
Stipends were paid in September 2023 to annuitants who met the qualifying age requirement on or
before August 31, 2023:

+ A one-time $7,500 stipend to 75 years of age and older eligible annuitants

+ A one-time $2,400 stipend to 70 to 74 years of age eligible annuitants
