NOTE 11: RISK MANAGEMENT
The District is exposed to various risks of loss related to torts, theft of, damage to and destruction
of assets, errors and omissions, injuries to employees, and natural disasters. The District's risk
management program encompasses various means of protecting the District against losses
through policies with commercial insurance carriers or through self-insurance.
Workers’ Compensation
The District maintains a self-insurance program for workers’ compensation. Contributions are
paid from all governmental and proprietary funds to the Workers’ Compensation Internal Service
Fund from which all claims and administrative expenses are paid. Claims administration, loss
control, and consultant services are provided by a third-party administrator. The District maintains
a catastrophic loss insurance policy for catastrophic losses exceeding $1,000,000 per occurrence
up to statutory limit of liability.
