November 10, 2025
The Houston Independent School District presents the Budget Book for the 2025-2026 fiscal year
to the Board of Education and relevant stakeholders.
The Board of Managers formally adopted the budget on June 12, 2025, for the fiscal year
commencing July 1, 2025 and concluding June 30, 2026.
The district's adopted 2025-2026 budget anticipates an overall net revenue decline of $141.4
million compared to the 2024-2025 adopted budget. Local funding is expected to increase by
$2.6 million, reflecting a $26.7 million rise in property tax revenue, offset by decreases of $17
million in interest earnings and $7 million from other governmental sources. State sources are
projected to yield a net increase of $190.2 million, consisting primarily of a $182.6 million gain in
Foundation School Program funding and an additional $7.6 million in per capita state revenues.
Federal sources forecast a net increase of $560,000, with federal revenue from TEA-IDC up by
$330,000 and military-related federal revenue increasing by $230,000. Conversely, other operating
revenues are expected to decrease by $52 million, mainly due to property sales.
The 2025-2026 budget was developed using a tax rate of $1.0450 per $100 of taxable value,
encompassing a Maintenance and Operations (M&O) rate of $0.8783 and an Interest and Sinking
(1&S) rate of $0.1667.
Development, review, and approval of the 2025-2026 Governmental Funds—including the
General Fund, Nutrition Services Fund, Debt Service Fund, and Capital Projects Fund—were
conducted through comprehensive analysis of revenue and expenditure accounts. The Financial
Section of this document presents the budgets for the General Fund, Debt Service Fund, and
Nutrition Services Fund, which, as required by Texas law, must receive Board of Education
approval by function.
This budget book is crafted to satisfy the requirements for the Meritorious Budget Award (MBA)
of the Association of School Business Officials International (ASBO) and the Budget Award of the
Government Finance Officers Association (GFOA).
