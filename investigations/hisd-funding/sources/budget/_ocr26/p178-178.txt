By June 30, 2026, the Finance Department will earn all 4 targeted Texas Comptroller Transparency Stars—Traditional
Finances, Contracts & Procurement, Public Pensions, and Debt Obligations—by meeting the rigorous standards for
financial transparency established by the Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts. These awards recognize local
governments that go above and beyond in making their financial data accessible, clear, and meaningful to the public.
Each Transparency Star requires the district to:
*Post key financial documents and data in user-friendly formats
*Provide narrative explanations to help the public understand the context and significance of the financial
information.
*Meet specific benchmarks and content requirements tailored to each area of recognition, such as detailed revenue
and expenditure reports, procurement procedures, pension funding data, and debt service obligations.
