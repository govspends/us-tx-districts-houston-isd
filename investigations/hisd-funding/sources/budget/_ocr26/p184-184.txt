Chief of Staff
Department Description
The Chief of Staff department is a key part of the superintendent cabinet, working to align district
strategy, operations, and communications, supporting the superintendent's vision, and bridging
leadership with school-level execution.
Key Action 1 - Improve Districtwide systems and efficiency - Data Goverance

data.
o By January 2026, the 400 queries have codified business rules, allowing any employee with

'400 questions’.
