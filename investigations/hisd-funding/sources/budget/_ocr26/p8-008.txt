It should be noted that a criterion from the MBA program regarding alternative tax collections
does not apply to the district, as HISD’s tax collections are restricted to local property taxes. Texas
school districts are not authorized to levy taxes beyond property taxes, such as sales tax.
This adopted budget reflects strategic allocation of resources to support the Board's goals, the
Destination 2035 vision, and the district's action plan.
In Houston ISD, zip code no longer dictates destiny. On August 12th no HISD student walked into
an F-rated campus. 70,000 more students walked into A- and B-rated campuses than the year
prior. Over the last two years, HISD has more than doubled the number of A- and B-rated schools,
improving opportunities and outcomes for students across the city. HISD is not stopping there.
Our pursuit is for every child to attend a high-quality school and for HISD to be the highest
performing large urban district in the nation. By August 2027, all HISD schools will be A- or B-
rated. That is the Houston Promise.
