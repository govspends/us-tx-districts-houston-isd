Key Action 4 - Expand access to high-quality post-secondary pathways
Indicators of Success
o Academic Readiness: By June 2026, the number of 11th and 12th grade students who complete the 2025-26
academic year TSI ready in English and Math (SAT/ACT/TSIA/TCB) will increase 5%.
o Advanced Coursework: The number of 11th grade students earning college credit will increase 5% from June of 2025
to June of 2026.
o Career (Retention): By June 2026, there will be a 5% increase in the percent of students who are currently
participants, explorers and concentrators continuing in their program of study sequence.
o Career (Success): By June 2026, 70% of students enrolled in CTE program of study course with a required Industry-
Based Certifications (IBCs) will have earned at least one certification.
o College: By June 2026, there will be a 5% increase in seniors who are TSI-Ready or CTE Completers accepted at post-
secondary institutions.
o Career (Selection): By June 2026, there will be a 25% increase in the percent of 9th graders enrolled in one of the
approved high-wage, high-demand programs of study.
o Artificial Intelligence (Al): By May 2026, increase the number of students taking the “Al in the Workplace” elective
by 5%.
