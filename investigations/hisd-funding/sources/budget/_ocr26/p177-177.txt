Chief of Finance
Department Description
The Finance Department manages all district financial operations—budgeting, accounting, payroll, purchasing, and
reporting. Oversees the Controller’s Office, Budgeting & Financial Planning, Payroll, and Purchasing Services. Ensures fiscal
health, processes tax revenues and vendor payments, manages investments, and provides financial transparency.
Specialized teams handle capital projects and Medicaid revenue.
Key Action 1 - Improve Districtwide Systems and Efficiency
Indicators of Success
o Maintain a final fund balance of $810 million +/- 10% by end of FY2026, based on fiscal trend analysis and aligned
with the prior year’s fund balance (currently $1,047,196,700), ensuring long-term financial sustainability.
By the end of each quarter, Quarterly Financial Reports (Budget to Actual) will be completed and presented to the
Board in a timely and consistent manner, reflecting accurate and transparent financial performance across the
e district. Each report will include a comprehensive analysis of expenditures, with targeted benchmarks of
approximately 25% by Q1, 50% by Q2, 75% by Q3, and less than 100% by Q4, allowing for strategic year-end budget
management.
By the end of each month, the Finance Department, including division and central office finance teams, will hold
structured monthly meetings with key stakeholders to review budget variances. These meetings will provide an
® opportunity to analyze financial trends, address discrepancies, and offer guidance on budget adjustments.
Additionally, Finance will collaborate with departments to enhance their understanding of financial reports, ensuring
they have the tools and knowledge to make informed decisions.
By the end of each month, Division Finance teams will maintain regular, collaborative meetings with their assigned
PUA campuses to support campus leaders in understanding, monitoring, and adjusting their budgets. These ongoing
® touchpoints create space for reviewing expenditures, addressing budget variances, and answering campus-specific
financial questions through technical assistance, hands-on walkthroughs of financial systems, and tailored guidance
sessions on interpreting variance reports and planning adjustments.
Specific Actions
1 CFO and Budgeting will monitor district-wide budget variances on a monthly basis through the development and
review of a standardized “Book of Numbers” report.
2 CFO and Payroll will monitor payroll on a monthly basis and examine APR to payroll checks.
3 CFO and Treasurer will monitor cash flows and forecasting end of the year for investment purposes each month.
4 CFO and Budgeting will monitor all fund balances (all funds) each month.
5 The Finance Department will meet monthly with the CFO to present and discuss the report findings, identify potential
concerns, and recommend necessary budget adjustments or follow-up actions.
The FASD department in collaboration with IT, HR, and FMO will host quarterly CFO meetings that include both PUA
6 and NES campus Office Managers and campus leadership. These sessions provide system-wide updates on financial
policies, budget timelines, and procedural changes, ensuring consistent communication, alignment, and capacity
building across all autonomous campuses.
7 To support maintaining a target fund balance of $810 million, the team will monitor expenditure controls, refine
revenue forecasting, and ensure structural balance in proposed budgets.
