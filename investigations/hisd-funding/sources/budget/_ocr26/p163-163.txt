Chief of Public Affairs
The Public Affairs & Communications Department manages all internal/external messaging, media,
board relations, community engagement, and crisis communication. This department works to build
public trust, and communicate district changes, utilizing channels like social media, newsletters, and
public information requests (PIOs) to ensure transparency, with recent focus on district transformation
and stakeholder engagement.
