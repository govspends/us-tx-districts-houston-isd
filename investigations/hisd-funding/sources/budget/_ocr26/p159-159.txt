Chief of Schools
Department Description
The Chief of Schools department oversees the district's geographic divisions, acting as a key link between the
superintendent and the Division Chiefs to ensure consistent, high-quality instruction, curriculum, and support for
all schools. This department implements data-driven strategies, standardizes key initiatives, and improves efficiency
by reducing redundancies across divisions, creating one unified vision for the entire district.
Key Action 1 - Improve the Quality of Instruction
Indicators of Success
® The District will average at least a 10 for the February IRT and an 11.5 on the end-of-year IRT
. All potential IRT evaluators will have passed the TES appraiser certification to ensure strong calibration before
being allowed to conduct an IRT visit.
® At least 80% of all principals receive a “Proficient” score on the instructional feedback rubric by 30 May 2026
® 20% of all teachers apply for a Distinguished Teacher Review by 31 March 2026
® The average teacher applying for DTR review receives at least 10 additional points from the DTR review
® February IRT score of at least 200 SPED teachers at or above 10.0 and EOY at or above 11.5
® At least 200 SPED teachers in HISD will average at or above proficient | on the spot observation tool by EOY
. The District's average mid-year IRT score of at least 200 Pre-K teachers at or above 9.0. This increases to 10.5
by the end-of-year. (request 8.5 midyear, and 9.5 eoy)
® At least 200 Pre-K teachers in HISD will average at or above proficient | on the spot observation tool by EOY
By May 2026, there will be a 5% increase in the number of Pre-K students who meet their target growth goal
from BOY to EOY as compared to SY24-25.
Specific Actions
1 EDIs will visit each of their campuses at least once a week, and twice a week for priority schools, to calibrate
and support school leadership in increasing HQl occurrences.
2 EDIs will analyze spot data biweekly to ensure a high level of calibration between the school and IRT
3 Divison Chiefs will lead calibration walks with EDIs for a minimum of six visits per week to provide coaching,
guidance on implementing the instructional strategies trained by the Superintendent and COS
4 COS Office will conduct random IRT walks on focus schools to support HQI consistently
5 COS Office will train EDI's and Principals on HQI including, but not limited to, Miles Six Core Components of
Excellence and the Great Eight
6 COS Office will train all EDI's and Principals on the IRT process including calibrations and certification
assessment
7 COS will train EDI's and principals on the Instructional Feedback Rubric
8 COS to train on the Teacher and Principal evaluation system including the DTR process and timeline
9 COS to invite and support the application process to DTR for all teachers who are invited to apply via data
points
