Chief of Human Resources
The Human Resources department manages recruitment, employee relations, and talent development,
offering support via the HR Help portal , Employee Services and a dedicated Benefits line for all
personnel needs from hiring to retirement.
Indicators of Success

additional Pre-K seats added by September 1, 2025.
o By end of January 2025, HR will recruit, hire, and onboard qualified teachers to fully staff the 300
additional seats added by February 1, 2026.

February 2026.
