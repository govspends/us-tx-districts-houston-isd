Department Description
Office of Legal Services handles district legal matters, including litigation, employment law, student
rights, contracts, and compliance with laws like the Public Information Act and Title IX. The office
provides counsel on various educational legal issues and serves employees with claims arising from their
job duties for a goal of consistent, data-driven learning.
