General Fund Revenues
The adopted budget reflects the allocation of revenues and appropriations to support educational programs
and services defined by the district's purpose, goals, strategic intent, and core values. Projected revenues
for the 2025-2026 General Fund are $2,127,033,202 which is a 7.1 percent increase over the final 2024-2025
budgeted revenues. Revenues for the General Fund and other financing sources are primarily generated
through local property taxes and state aid, but include other sources. A description of the methodology for
calculating state formula revenue in detail is included in the Informational Section of this document.
General Fund Revenue Assumptions
The 2025-2026 state revenue projections are based on an estimated average daily attendance (ADA) of
151,666 pupils, or weighted average daily attendance (WADA) of 223,312.
Property tax revenue is the district's largest revenue source in the amount of $1,563,545,630 comprising of
77% of all budgeted revenues. HISD is projecting an increase in local funding from the 2024-2025 original
budgeted property tax levy of $26.7 million, an increase of state funding of $190.2 million, and an increase
$560 thousand in federal sources, for a net increase of $193.4 million in revenues
