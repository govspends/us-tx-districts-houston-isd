Chief of Police
Department Description
HISD Police is a full-service police department consisting of 24-hour patrol, criminal investigations, and
community outreach and emergency response teams. Their jurisdiction encompasses more than 300
square miles of district property. They are committed to maintaining an environment in which students
feel safe to learn, employees feel safe to work, and the community has confidence in the safety of the
district.
