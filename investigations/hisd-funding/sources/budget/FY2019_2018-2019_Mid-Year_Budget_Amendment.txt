GENERAL FUND

      The recommended budget for the General Fund for the 2018-2019 fiscal year was adopted on June 25, 2018. The appropriations
      were $2,043,345,624, with corresponding estimated revenues of $2,007,745,003. The district projected use of $18,436,625 from the
      assigned reserve for Public Facility Corporation payments resulting in a deficit of $17,163,996 for the fiscal year.

      Mid-Year Budgetary Update

      Revenues

      The adjusted budget for the General Fund revenues and other financing sources includes a net increase of $88,121,194 from the
      original budget, as shown in more detail on page 5.

      The major changes in revenue are listed below:

      Increases

              Increase of $16,817,372 in Current Year Taxes
              Increase of $40,000,000 in Teacher Retirement System (TRS) On Behalf, this is offset by the same amount in TRS On Behalf
               expenditures
              Increase of $17,034,739 in capital lease proceeds, this is offset by the same amount in capital lease expenditures
              State sources increase of $3,037,613 in Foundation School Program
              Federal sources experienced an increase of $5,582,309 due to the reassignment of the Build America Bond Subsidies
               (BABS) from the debt service fund to the general fund
              Receipt of $8,518,889 for Harvey insurance claims
              Increase of $1,519,273 in Federal revenue through TEA

      Decreases

              Decrease of $4,400,000 in transfers from the Medicaid Fund
              Decrease of other state revenues of $1,140,000

    Appropriations

    Appropriations show a net increase of $151,381,307 as shown in more detail on page 4.

    The major changes in appropriations are listed below:

            Items approved during 2018-2019 by the HISD Board of Education in the amount of $3,252,703. Details are listed below:
                 o $4,331,902 for teacher step salary increase
                 o $450,000 for legal settlement
                 o $1,529,199 appropriation reduction for Kandy Stripe closure

    Increases

            Carryover encumbrances of $81,731,396 which includes funds reservation and ERP projects
            TRS on behalf expenses in the amount of $40,000,000
            Capital lease recognition in the amount of $17,034,739
            Achieve 180 budget increase in the amount of $4,303,151
            Wrap Around budget increase in the amount of $6,483,724
            State Compensatory Education expenditures in the amount of $9,641,117
            Transportation for the 4 Hurricane Harvey rebuild schools in the amount of $4,053,000
            Charter school enrollment funding increase in the amount of $2,727,968
            Self-insurance budget increase in the amount of $1,000,000

    Decreases

            Recapture payment reduction in the amount of $11,017,296 primarily due to a provision in the Texas Education Code which
             allows the Texas Education Agency (TEA) commissioner to reduce recapture to offset unreimbursed Hurricane Harvey costs
            Campus PUA enrollment decline in the amount of $8,083,444

    Budget

    Final budgeted revenues and other sources total $2,095,866,197. The appropriations budget for the General Fund is $2,194,726,930
    including carryover. Taking into account both assigned and unassigned reserve adjustments, and changes in revenues and
    appropriations, the district’s budgeted unassigned fund balance will increase by an additional $18,471,283 resulting in a surplus of
    $1,307,287.

      The recommended budget for the Debt Service Fund for the 2018-2019 fiscal year was adopted on June 25, 2018.

      Mid-Year Budgetary Update

      Appropriations

NUTRITION SERVICES FUND

         The recommended budget for the Nutrition Services Fund for the 2018-2019 fiscal year was adopted on June 25,
         2018. Revenues and appropriations and were estimated at $142,147,259 and $139,945,266, respectively.

         Mid-Year Budgetary Update

        Revenues

         Due to the drop in student enrollment resulting in decreased overall participation, total revenues are forecasted to
         decrease $3,416,727. The Child and Adult Care Program is expected to show the biggest decline due to lower
         enrollment and fewer ASP/Enrichment programs. While miscellaneous sources are expected to increase slightly
         ($175,953), we expect to see the following decreases in specific program revenues:

             •   School Breakfast Program - $1,045,300
             •   National School Lunch Program - $795,151
             •   Child and Adult Care Program - $1,290,103
             •   Fresh Fruit and Vegetable Program - $462,125

        Appropriations

         Appropriations are increasing $7,289,158 with the largest changes as follows:

             •   $3,448,377 increase in contracted maintenance and repair for custodial and HVAC contracts
             •   $3,697,189 increase in food and non-food expenditures as the result of the introduction of salad bars in
                 schools

        Budget

         The appropriations budget for the Nutrition Services Fund (as adjusted) is $147,234,424 with corresponding
         estimated revenue of $138,730,532. The district’s budgeted reserved fund balance will decrease by $10,705,885
         resulting in a deficit of $8,503,892.
