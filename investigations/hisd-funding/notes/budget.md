### Deficit drivers HISD itself cited

- **FY25:**
  - End of ESSER: $323.9M of ESSER in FY24 → $0 in FY25 (WS-May24 p24).
  - Property tax revenue fell $351.9M vs FY24's original budget because of state compression and higher homestead exemptions. This was offset by the end of recapture: FY24's original budget had **$326.5M of recapture** and FY25 had $0 (B25 p6, p110).
  - NES expansion to 130 schools.
- **FY26:** the adoption bridge (P26 p13-15).
  - Revenue +$175.4M: Basic Allotment and entitlements +$83.9M; Teacher Retention Allotment +$36.6M; Allotment for Basic Costs +$18.3M; Support Staff Retention +$6.3M; School Safety +$6.6M.
  - Cuts −$122.1M: departments −$29.2M, PUA campuses −$14.1M, NES campuses −$21.5M, one-time −$12.8M, recapture −$44.5M.
  - Additions +$67.8M: teacher pay raise $11.7M, support staff raise $5.8M, SPED staff $13.7M, Pre-K $5.0M, charters/DAEP/JJAEP $10.9M, maintenance tax notes $4.0M, etc.
- **FY27:** the bridge (WS-May26 slide 14).
  - Revenue −$38.4M: enrollment loss −$50M (4,000 students); loss of the "disaster relief tax" pennies −$50M; Teacher Incentive Allotment +$27.5M; property sales +$35M; 1882-partnership pass-through −$24M.
  - Cuts −$124M: central office −$35M, NES decreases −$29M, 1882 partnerships −$19M, school consolidation −$15M, vacancies −$15M, "efficiencies" −$12M, one-time −$6M, overtime −$3M.
  - Increases +$61.5M: PUA/PFP campus pay +$11M; electricity & water +$13.5M; central office salaries +$7.75M and minimum-wage raise +$5M; Future 2 $4.5M; health insurance $4M.
  - **Self-insured health fund:** projected net position −$19.5M (FY26 estimate) and −$5.5M (FY27). This is the reason for the plan consolidation from 7 plans to 4 (WS-May26 slides 41, 48).

## 7. Campus allocation model (how money gets to schools)

### Two systems

| | NES | Non-NES ("PUA", Per-Unit Allocation; renamed EAS, "Earned Autonomy System", May 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Who builds the budget | Built **centrally** from standardized staffing models (teacher apprentices, learning coaches, SPED aides); principals have little discretion | Principal-built from a per-student formula |
| Formula | Staffing crosswalks (WS-Apr25 pp25-26) | Projected enrollment × attendance (ADA%) × per-unit allocation (WS-May24 p29; WS-Apr25 p13) |

**Year-by-year formula changes:**

- **FY25:** "hold harmless" for enrollment and attendance was eliminated, with loss capped at 12% (B25 p7; WS-May24 p29).
- **FY26:** +$75/student, with 10% of any loss added back (WS-Apr25 p12).
- **FY27:** a new "PPOR" (Per Pupil Operating Revenue) formula (WS-Apr26 slides 42-44; WS-May26 slides 17-33):
  - $1,400 base per student × projected enrollment × ADA;
  - special-population weights aligned to the state's (at-risk, SPED, G/T, CTE, EL, refugee, homeless);
  - teacher salaries funded at actual pay-for-performance levels × 1.15 benefits;
  - magnet allocation;
  - a floor and ceiling: no campus loses more than 1% or gains more than 4% vs its FY26 allocation.
  - NES schools get the same formula plus **+$500 per pupil "academic need"** (WS-May26 slide 20-21 diagram text).

**Autonomy tiers (FY25 book):** Level 4 Separate & Unique magnets (21 schools); Level 3 A/B-rated (57); Level 2 C/D-rated non-NES (41); Level 1 NES (130); special (18); total 267 (B25 p58, image).

## 9. Inconsistencies and errors in HISD's own documents

1. **Tax rate misstatements:**
   - B25's transmittal letter says the budget used "$1.0350 … M&O $0.8683" (p6). The body says $0.8683 total = 0.7016 M&O + 0.1667 I&S (p19, p119).
   - B26's letter says "$1.0450 … M&O $0.8783" (p7 OCR). The body says $0.8783 total (p21, p123).
   - The letters are wrong.
2. **Fund balance typo:** B25 p6 and p91 write the FY24 fund balance as "$1.047 million"; it should read billion.
3. **Unassigned FB repeated:** B25 p91 shows unassigned FB of $590,067,862 for both 6/30/2023 and 6/30/2024. `notes/acfr.md` confirms this is what the ACFRs report; FY24's drawdown was absorbed by the instructional-reserve assignment.
4. **Recapture FY25:** three HISD figures ($44.5M final budget, $56.97M Dec amendment, $56.9M actual) vs TEA's $49.1M. The FY25 adopted budget assumed $0.
5. **FY26 state FSP:** the narrative says FSP is "approximately $110.5 million" (B26 p88, p113). The revenue comparison table says Foundation Fund $238,160,263 (B26 p114 OCR). The table total ($414.5M) ties to S26, so the narrative likely omits other state programs.
6. **FY26 GF appropriation:** B26 p25 (OCR) shows $2,121,877,295 vs $2,121,893,114 in S26. The $15,819 difference equals function 81.
7. **FY26 Debt Service:** B26 p25 calls $389,749,309 the "Debt Service Fund budget"; that is revenue. Appropriations are $397,003,334.
8. **FY26 beginning FB:** adopted at $799.9M (S26). The book shows $750.4M (B26 p33). The audited figure is $748.1M. The adopted budget overstated starting reserves by about $52M because the FY25 actual deficit (about $297–299M) far exceeded the amended −$247M.
9. **FY25 ADA:** B25 p18 calls 179,592 the ADA; it is HISD's enrollment count.
10. **FY27 May-20 preliminary vs adopted Debt Service:** local revenue was $369.3M in the May 20 preliminary and $383.0M as adopted; ending DS FB moved $159.5M → $173.2M. The GF did not change between the preliminary and adoption.
